# Bora Campo — product direction

Bora Campo serves sports arenas, schools and training centers. It combines arena operations (spaces, reservations, services, classes, students and tuition) with local discovery through public profiles and marketplace. Initial market Manaus. Audience: owners and staff who need reliable operational control and players who need fast mobile discovery. The demo illustrates the intended workflow but must not be confused with released functionality. Commercial plans are proposals until entitlement, subscription and gateway workflows are ready. Visual character: pragmatic, confident, sports-oriented without sports clichés; calm neutrals and purposeful green. Prioritize speed, trust, useful information and clear actions. No fictitious evidence or promised integrations.
